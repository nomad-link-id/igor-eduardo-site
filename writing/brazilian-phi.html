Brazilian-PHI

Igor Eduardo · 10 September 2026 · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20076543

The public Brazilian-PHI work is the first benchmark for detecting Brazilian PII in Portuguese clinical text — seven identifier types with mod-11 checksum validation. This note restates only what is already on the site and in the Zenodo record.

The research page names the artifact in those terms. The companion preprint is deposited at DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20076543, titled Brazilian-PHI: Benchmarking Checksum-Validated Recognizers for CPF, CRM, CNS, CNPJ, RG, CEP, and Phone in Portuguese Clinical Text (Eduardo, 2026; Zenodo).

What the record reports

The Zenodo record states that the seven Brazilian PII types covered by the benchmark are CPF, CRM, CNS, CNPJ, RG, CEP, and phone numbers. The evaluation uses custom Presidio recognizers with mod-11 checksum validation, compared with default Presidio and Groq Llama 3.3 70B, across 500 synthetic Portuguese clinical notes (670 PII instances, 1,940 distractors).

As published in that record: custom Presidio achieves macro F1=1.000 versus 0.270 for default Presidio (McNemar chi2=313.003, p<0.001) and is 80,000x faster than Llama 3.3. Checksum validation deterministically eliminates false positives for CPF and CNPJ. The record states that all code and corpus are released open-source.

Public artifacts

The paper is paired with a public repository, linked from the homepage. The Zenodo record also lists a Hugging Face dataset as a related identifier. This note does not add claims beyond those public sources.
