Who is Igor Eduardo

Igor Eduardo is an AI engineer, applied researcher, and founder based in Austin, Texas.

Igor Eduardo is an AI engineer, applied researcher, and technology founder based in Austin, Texas. His work focuses on clinical AI, pharmacovigilance, retrieval systems, and healthcare intelligence.

He is the founder of Cortexa Social, Inc., a Delaware C-corp established in 2026, and of DocMinds. On this site, Cortexa is an independent research capability: computational pipelines, model training, and real-world-evidence work from public data sources. DocMinds is a clinical AI platform for regulated healthcare.

He trains and evaluates models — including on-device inference on Apple Silicon — designs retrieval and data pipelines, and publishes the reasoning when the work is public. His ORCID iD is 0009-0005-6288-1135.

Before that, he founded and exited a technology company, then spent seven years in venture investing across healthcare, fintech, and SaaS. He is an acquired founder of Kubrick.
